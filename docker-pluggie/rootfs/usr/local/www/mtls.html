Client Certificate Required

This address only accepts devices with a client certificate.

What happened?

The owner of this address requires a client certificate, and this device did not present a valid one.

How to fix it

1

Open the Pluggie app and go to Settings → Client certificates.

2

Add this device and install the downloaded certificate on it.

3

Open this address again.

Locked out? Open the Pluggie app from your local network and switch Client certificates to Off. Local access is never affected.
